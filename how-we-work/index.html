How we work

Every claim gets checked. Every source gets shown.

What we make

  • Verdict (Mon): a viral health claim, judged against the research: SCIENCE, PARTLY or HYPE.
  • Guide (Wed): a real-life problem, why it matters, and the habits the evidence supports.
  • Supplement File (Fri): one supplement: what it does, who may need it, how strong the evidence is, and safety.
  • Carousels (Tue, Thu, Sat): one topic, one point per slide, a source on every slide.

How we read research

  • We prefer reviews, meta-analyses and randomised trials in humans over single small studies.
  • We say how big an effect is, not just whether one exists. "Modest" and "we don't know yet" are allowed answers.
  • We point out when a number depends on a definition (like a deficiency cut-off) or comes from a small or short study.
  • Every claim shows its source on screen, and full references are listed.

Rules we don't break

  • No disease claims. Food and supplements don't "cure" or "reverse" conditions.
  • No fear-selling, no fake urgency, no AI images of real people.
  • Honest verdicts even when the evidence is weak, including on things people love.
  • If we ever sell our own products, we'll say so clearly, and show the same evidence we'd show for anyone else's.

Not medical advice

We explain research so you can make better everyday choices. We're not your doctor. If you have a health condition, take medication or are pregnant, talk to a qualified doctor before changing anything.
