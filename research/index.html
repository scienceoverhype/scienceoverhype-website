SUPPLEMENT FILE · #04

Should you take a vitamin D supplement?

PARTLY

Low vitamin D is common in India, but in the 5-year VITAL trial, 2,000 IU a day didn't meaningfully cut cancer or heart disease in mostly healthy adults. Test, fix a real deficiency, don't megadose.

VERDICT · #02

Does it take 21 days to form a habit?

HYPE

No. The 21-day rule comes from a 1960 self-help book. In a UCL study of 96 people, habits took 66 days on average to become automatic, from 18 to about 254 days.
