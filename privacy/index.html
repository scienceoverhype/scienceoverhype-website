Privacy policy

This policy explains how Science Over Hype ("we", "us") handles your personal data when you use scienceoverhype.com or sign up for our emails.

What we collect

  • When you sign up: your first name (optional) and email address, plus the date and the page you signed up from.
  • When you read our emails: whether an email was opened and which links were clicked, so we can improve our content.
  • When you write to us: whatever you choose to include in your message.

We don't collect payment details, health records or sensitive personal data through this website.

Why we use it

  • To send you the free guide you asked for and the emails you agreed to receive.
  • To understand which content is useful, and to reply when you write to us.

We use your data only with your consent, which you give by ticking the box when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who processes it

Our email list is managed by MailerLite (MailerLite UAB), which stores and sends emails on our behalf under its own security and privacy standards. We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you're subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we stop emailing you, and you can ask us to delete your data completely.

Your rights

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you.
  • Raise a concern with us about how your data is handled.

Contact

Email thescienceoverhype@gmail.com for any privacy request. We aim to respond within 7 days.
